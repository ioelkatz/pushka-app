Privacy Policy — Pushka

Last updated: January 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pushka ("the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Usage data: basic interactions with the App (e.g., creating reminders, donations, transaction history).
  • Device data: device type, language, and operating system.
  • Notifications: tokens used to send push notifications.
  • Payments: payment data is processed by Stripe and is not stored in the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process donations and display transaction history.
  • To schedule and send reminders and notifications.
  • To improve the user experience and stability of the App.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to operate the App:

  • Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics).
  • Stripe (payment processing).

These providers may collect data in accordance with their own policies.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only when:

  • It is necessary for the operation of the App (e.g., Stripe).
  • There is a legal obligation to do so.

5. Retention and Deletion

We retain data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion by writing to support@pushkaapp.com, or delete your account directly from the app (Settings → Delete account).

6. Security

We apply reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no system is 100 % secure.

7. User Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by writing to support@pushkaapp.com. The app also lets you export your data at any time from Settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will publish the new version in the App or on this website.
