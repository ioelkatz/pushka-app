## LONG DESCRIPTION

Pushka is the official Colel Chabad app — your tzedakah charity box, always in your pocket.

Just like a physical pushka accumulates coins until it's ready to be emptied, this app lets you add small donations throughout the week and send them to Colel Chabad with a single tap — securely, and with a full record of every contribution.

**What can you do with Pushka?**

🪙 **Fill your Pushka**
Tap preset amounts to accumulate tzedakah. Set a goal and celebrate your mitzvah when you reach it.

💳 **Empty and donate**
When you're ready, empty your Pushka and send the full amount directly to Colel Chabad via secure card payment.

🔔 **Smart reminders**
Set up daily, weekly, or Jewish holiday reminders — Erev Shabbat, Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, and more — so you never miss your mitzvah.

📊 **Full history**
Keep a detailed log of every donation and top-up with dates and amounts.

💰 **Integrated wallet**
Top up your wallet, send tzedakah to contacts, and manage your funds effortlessly.

⚙️ **Auto-empty**
Schedule your Pushka to empty automatically at the frequency you choose: weekly, monthly, or Erev Rosh Chodesh.

🔐 **Security and privacy**
Optional biometric authentication for donations. Your data is protected with Firebase and end-to-end encryption for all payments.

🌍 **Multilingual**
Available in Español, English, Français, and עברית.

**About Colel Chabad**
Founded in 1788, Colel Chabad is Israel's oldest continuously operating charity. It feeds thousands of families in need, supports widows and orphans, and provides medical and social assistance across the country.

Every coin in your Pushka makes a real difference.
